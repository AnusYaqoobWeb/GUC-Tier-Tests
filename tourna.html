RULES

Match Format: All matches are Best of 3 (BO3). First team to win 2 maps advances. Losing team is eliminated. Grand Finals are Best of 5 (BO5).

Bracket Structure: 10 teams compete in a single-elimination bracket. The top 6 seeds receive a first-round bye and enter directly in the Quarter Finals. Seeds #7-#10 play in the Preliminary Round.

Prize: €10 Gift Card awarded to the champion.

ABOUT THE TOURNAMENT

The 4v4 Arena is a community-driven competitive gaming platform designed for squads of four. This tournament brings together 10 elite teams battling through a single-elimination bracket for the €10 Gift Card prize.

Participating teams include RTSK, ukp9omegaalex, IVqzcain77, pinkysss, Report Us Later, MeInJapan, KewJillers, MommyAktivesDomination, PuionFire, and Skill Isuee.

Organized by Esports Community. For inquiries join our Discord: discord.gg/4v4tournament
